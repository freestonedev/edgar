About

How hard can it be?

That's the question I've built a career on. Not "how hard is it?", which is how projects die in planning, but "how hard can it be?", which is how a marketer ends up building publishing pipelines, GEO programs, and multi-agent automation. I've spent 14 years turning that curiosity into shipped work across marketing, product, and program management, and AI has removed whatever ceiling was left: the distance between "I wonder if" and "it's live" has never been shorter.

Here's what I believe about this moment: the most influential people in the AI era aren't the developers. They're the subject matter experts who use AI, the marketer, the editor, the operator who knows exactly what needs to exist and can now build it themselves. Everything on this site is that belief in practice.

I partnered with Y Combinator founders to build a lead-generation engine that captured 4,000+ software developers across Latin America, and I work for them today as senior program manager for US marketing. With AI in the toolkit my lane keeps widening: GTM programs, web operations, the content pipeline, GEO measurement, and reporting automation, work that has contributed to over 700% website traffic growth in under two years. Before that I grew Moonshine Rods' organic blog traffic by 400% in one year, and cut my teeth running creative programs at Musicbed and Filmsupply.

If you're building a web platform for the AI era, or trying to figure out what GEO actually means for your brand, I'd love to compare notes.

When I'm not shipping, I'm outside.

I'm a certified Orvis-Endorsed fly fishing guide based in Colorado, and an avid off-roader and overlander. Most weekends you'll find me on a river or a trail, which is not an accident: it's the same instinct that shapes how I work. Guiding teaches you to read conditions, adapt the plan, and put the person you're with in a position to succeed. Overlanding teaches you that preparation and good tooling are the difference between an adventure and a rescue.

It's also why the outdoor work in this portfolio exists. Moonshine Rods and Flylords hired someone who fishes their rivers, not just someone who markets to the people who do. You can see for yourself on Instagram at @e.dgr.
