What this page is

Two quick definitions. A Claude skill is a reusable, documented AI workflow: instructions, rules, and quality guardrails packaged so the work runs the same way every time, for anyone. MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard that connects an AI to real tools: analytics, spreadsheets, project boards, calendars. Skills are the recipes; MCP is the plumbing. I've built the recipes and installed the plumbing, and everything below is in active use.

Custom skills I've authored

The Tech Leadership Podcast publishing suite
Orchestrator

A tool that auto-publishes new guest profiles from one command: 51 pages live and counting

Hand it a raw interview transcript and it runs the entire pipeline: delegates the writing, cleaning, and deploying to the specialized skills below, pauses only for human approval checkpoints, and finishes with the guest live on the site.

Skill: publish-bio · transcript in, live page out
Content generation

A tool that writes publish-ready web copy from every new interview recorded in SF and Austin: 74 transcripts and counting

Reads the interview and produces every publish-ready field in one pass: multi-paragraph bio, emphasis-marked headline, notable quote, role, company, category, and URL slug, all in a consistent editorial voice.

Skill: guest-bio · every field, one pass
Content production

A tool that turns a raw interview transcript into an SEO-ready web page in minutes, not hours

Strips timestamps, chit-chat, and filler, restores real speaker names, then bakes the cleaned conversation into the guest's page as static HTML that search engines and AI answer engines can read.

Skill: transcript-clean · SEO-ready long-form
Build & deploy

A tool that lets the team update the live website from a spreadsheet: 481 deploys in 4 months

Builds guest pages from the live content sheet and pushes them to GitHub, scoping each deploy to just the intended guests so nothing unrelated gets swept into a release. The manual override for the scheduled build.

Skill: build-bio-pages · scoped, safe deploys
Content intelligence

A searchable library that remembers and updates 70+ interviews (and counting)

Mines each new interview for its strongest verbatim, attributed, timecoded quotes, tags them by theme in a shared bank, and marks lines as used after a video ships so nothing repeats. The archive's memory.

Skill: quote-bank · read once, reuse forever
Video production

A tool that turns a 70+ interview archive into weeks of news-pegged social video

Matches the day's industry news against the quote bank and outputs a complete package: quick-cut montage script, editor's clip-pull sheet with timecodes, LinkedIn post copy, and a fact-check of the news peg.

Skill: news-cut · news to script in minutes

These aren't six separate tools; they're one multi-agent publishing system. The orchestrator (publish-bio) delegates to specialized workers, one writes, one cleans, one deploys, each with its own guardrails, and the finished pages publish themselves to GitHub, where an automated build takes them live. No human touches HTML anywhere in the chain; people only approve. This system is live and working right now: 481 automated builds in four months, new interviews recording every week, and the count still climbing.

Team & program management

  • ClickUp - task and program management, wired in so status updates write themselves.
  • Slack - reporting summaries and updates delivered where the team already lives.
  • Google Calendar - scheduling context for program timelines.

Outreach

  • Email and LinkedIn outreach platforms - campaign management and reporting for outbound programs, connected to the same reporting rhythm as everything else.

The pattern across all of it: connect the tool once, encode the process as a skill, and let the AI do the ferrying between systems that people used to do by hand.
